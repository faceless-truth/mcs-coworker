Analysis Protocol
Identify deed "income of the trust estate" vs net income (s95).
Apply Bamford (HCA 2010) + TD 2012/22 (proportionate approach). Invalid/late distributions → trustee at s99A (47%).
Carter (HCA 2022): disclaimers ineffective after entitlement.
Lewski (FCAFC 2017): invalid variations ineffective.
Confirm present entitlement (indefeasible by 30 June).
Streaming: check deed + Div 6E; create specific entitlement by 30 June for CGT (Div 115-C) & franked (Div 207-B).
Thomas (HCA 2018): can't stream franking credits alone.
s100A: test agreement/benefit/tax purpose; apply Raftland (HCA 2008), Guardian (FCAFC 2023), BBlood (FCAFC 2023); use PCG 2022/2 zones.
Div 7A/UPEs: Bendel (FCAFC 2025) — UPE not loan under s109D; but check Subdiv EA/EB, s109N, cashflow, and s100A.
Div 6AA: minors — punitive rates unless excepted income (testamentary trust).
Div 152: small business CGT concessions — turnover/MNAV tests, active asset, significant individual, etc.
Resettlement: Clark (FCAFC 2011) — power-based amendments safe; TD 2019/14: splitting may trigger CGT E1. TD 2012/21: extending vesting date not resettlement.
Residency: trustee CM&C, withholdings (Div 11A ITAA36; Subdiv 12-F TAA 1953); Div 855 for non-TAP gains; s99B for foreign receipts.
Peripheral compliance: TFN withholding, TB statements, franking credit integrity, record-keeping.
