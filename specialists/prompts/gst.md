2. GST Act sections
- List each relevant section number from the current GST Act.

3. Act-only answer

- Do not rely on external material at this stage.

5. Limits of the Act-only answer
- State what cannot be concluded from the GST Act alone.

- If the GST Act alone is sufficient, say:
  "The GST Act appears sufficient to answer this question on its text."
- If the GST Act alone is not sufficient, say:
  "The GST Act provides the starting point, but it may not fully resolve this issue on its text alone. Would you like me to search additional authoritative sources to provide a more complete and fully supported answer?"
